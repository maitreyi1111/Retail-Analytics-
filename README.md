# Retail-Analytics-

🛒 Retail Analytics Case Study
📌 Overview
In the rapidly evolving retail sector, businesses continuously seek innovative strategies to stay ahead of competitors, improve customer satisfaction, and optimize operational efficiency. Data analytics plays a critical role in achieving these objectives.

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales, customer behavior, and product performance using SQL and data analytics techniques. The goal is to generate actionable insights that help businesses make data-driven decisions related to marketing strategies, customer segmentation, and inventory management.

Through this case study, we analyze sales transactions, customer profiles, and product inventory data to identify patterns and improve overall business performance.

🎯 Business Problem
The retail company has observed stagnant growth and declining customer engagement over recent quarters. Initial assessments suggest potential issues such as:
Variability in product performance
Ineffective customer segmentation
Lack of insights into customer purchasing behavior
The company aims to leverage its data to better understand sales trends, customer engagement, and purchasing patterns.

🔍 Key Business Questions
1️⃣ Product Performance Variability
Identify which products perform well and which do not.
This helps in improving inventory management and marketing strategies.

2️⃣ Customer Segmentation
Segment customers based on purchasing behavior to enable targeted marketing campaigns and improved customer satisfaction.

3️⃣ Customer Behavior Analysis
Analyze patterns such as repeat purchases and customer loyalty to improve customer retention strategies.

🎯 Objectives
Perform data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA) using SQL.
Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
Segment customers based on purchase behavior.
Analyze customer loyalty and repeat purchases.
Generate insights to support inventory optimization and marketing strategies.

👥 Customer Segmentation Logic
Customers are segmented based on the total quantity of products purchased.
Total Quantity Purchased	Customer Segment
0	No Orders
1 – 10	Low
10 – 30	Mid
> 30	High Value

📂 Dataset Tables
1️⃣ Sales Transaction
Contains records of all sales transactions.
Columns include:
Transaction ID
Customer ID
Product ID
Quantity Purchased
Transaction Date
Price

2️⃣ Customer Profiles
Contains demographic information about customers.
Columns include:
Customer ID
Age
Gender
Location
Join Date

3️⃣ Product Inventory
Contains product-related details and stock information.
Columns include:
Product ID
Product Name
Category
Stock Level
Price
